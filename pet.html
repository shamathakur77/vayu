How this works

Pavan is a character, not a person. Its mood and words come from plain rules applied to the numbers VAYU publishes each night. Nothing is written by an AI while you watch, and nothing is guessed. Hosted next to VAYU, this page reads the same data files as the site when you open it. If it can't reach them, it shows a saved snapshot and says so at the top.

AQI here is the PM2.5 part of the Indian AQI, so the official city AQI can read higher. Go-out advice follows the CPCB health messages for each AQI band. It is not medical advice. Live numbers: VAYU site. Code: github.com/shamathakur77/vayu.
