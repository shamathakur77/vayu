Method, honestly

Gradient boosting on each city's own history: recent PM2.5, weather forecasts, seasonality, and for Delhi, Pune and Nashik the satellite fire count upwind. A persistence model (tomorrow equals today) runs alongside as the floor; if we cannot beat it, the scoreboard will say so. Truth is the median of the city's official monitors via OpenAQ, with modeled reanalysis as a flagged fallback. Data: CPCB and data.gov.in, OpenAQ, NASA FIRMS, Open-Meteo. Full audit in the README.
